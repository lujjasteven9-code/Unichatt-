Privacy Policy

unichatt v3.0  •  Last updated: August 27, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how unichatt ("we", "our", "the app") collects, uses, and protects your information when you use our messaging and social platform. By using unichatt, you agree to the practices described below.

Information We Collect

Account Information

When you sign up, we collect information such as your name, email address, and/or phone number, depending on the sign-in method you choose (email, phone/OTP, or other supported providers).

 

Content You Share

We store the messages, media (photos, videos, voice notes), status updates, and profile information you send or upload through the app, so that the app can deliver and display them to you and the people you communicate with.

 

Voice & Video Rooms

When you join a voice or video room, your audio/video stream is transmitted in real time through our real-time communication provider (Agora) to other participants in that room. We do not permanently store voice or video call recordings unless a call is explicitly recorded and saved as a feature you choose to use.

 

Device & Usage Information

We may automatically collect limited technical information such as app version, device type, and general usage activity to help us maintain and improve the app.

How We Use Your Information
  • To create and manage your account
  • To deliver messages, media, and calls between users
  • To personalize your experience (themes, settings, preferences)
  • To maintain the security and integrity of the app
  • To respond to support requests and bug reports
  • To communicate important updates about the app
How We Store & Protect Your Data

Your data is stored using Firebase (Authentication, Firestore Database, and Cloud Storage), a secure infrastructure provided by Google. We take reasonable technical measures to protect your information from unauthorized access, alteration, or loss. However, no method of electronic storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Sharing of Information

We do not sell your personal information. Your data may be shared only with:

  • Other users, as necessary for core app functionality (e.g. your name/photo being visible to people you chat with)
  • Service providers that help us operate the app (e.g. Firebase/Google Cloud for storage, Agora for voice/video)
  • Authorities, if required by law
Advertising

unichatt is currently free to use and does not display third-party advertising. If this changes in the future, this Privacy Policy will be updated in advance to explain what advertising-related data, if any, would be collected.

Your Choices
  • You can edit your profile information at any time within the app
  • You can adjust notification and privacy-related settings within the app
  • You may contact us to request information about the data we hold about you
Children's Privacy

unichatt is not intended for children under the age of 13 (or the minimum age required in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under this age.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Continued use of unichatt after changes are posted means you accept the updated policy.

Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, contact us at support@unichatt.app.
